Internet services you reach with an ordinary APRS message. Pick one, fill it in, and Use puts it in the APRS Messaging form; tick Transmit now and send. Replies show up under Conversations.
For replies to reach you, register once by sending SMSGTE: #mynumber add followed by your own number.
Save room with a shortcut: send EMAIL-2 me name@example.com once, then use me as the address.
Membership lasts 12 hours. Common groups: HOTG (hams on the go), APRSTHURSDAY (Thursday net).
